Lesson 8: Decimal to Hexadecimal

Study the concept, review the worked example, then complete the five-question lesson check.

Concept

What you will learn

To convert decimal to hexadecimal, repeatedly divide by 16. Remainders 10–15 are written as A, B, C, D, E and F.

Worked example

Let's work through it

Example: 254 ÷ 16 gives remainders 14 and 15; E F read upward gives FE₁₆.
