Lesson 6: Decimal to Octal

Study the concept, review the worked example, then complete the five-question lesson check.

Concept

What you will learn

To convert decimal to octal, repeatedly divide by 8. Record the remainders and read them from bottom to top.

Worked example

Let's work through it

Example: 83 ÷ 8 gives remainders 3,2,1; therefore 83₁₀ = 123₈.
