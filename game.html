Number Quest

Clear five stages of number-system challenges. Each cleared stage awards an upgraded achievement badge. The final 50-mark challenge unlocks after all ten lessons and all five game stages are complete.

How Number Quest works

Number Quest is a five-stage practice game. Each stage has three short questions based on the lessons. Answer all three correctly (3/3) to clear the stage and earn its badge.

01AnswerRead each number-system question and enter your answer.
02Clear the stageYou need 3 correct answers out of 3. If you miss one, retry the stage.
03Earn a badgeEach cleared stage awards a new badge and unlocks the next stage.
04Unlock the final challengeClear all five stages and complete all ten lessons to unlock the 50-mark challenge.

Tip: You can use the lesson notes, PowerPoint slides, Practice Zone and Number System Tutor to revise before retrying.
